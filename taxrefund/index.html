How this is calculated. Tax saved from FHSA and RRSP contributions is the difference in combined federal and Ontario income tax with and without the deduction, using 2026 brackets, basic personal amounts and the Ontario surtax. The Home Buyers' Amount is a $10,000 federal credit worth $1,400 in 2026. Land transfer tax refunds are the Ontario first-time buyer refund (up to $4,000) and the City of Toronto refund (up to $4,475). FHSA contributions are capped at $8,000 a year plus one year of carry-forward; RRSP contributions need available room (18% of last year's earned income, up to $33,810). The federal GST relief on new homes is law for first-time buyers (full relief up to $1,000,000, phasing out to $1,500,000); Ontario's 8% first-time buyer rebate has been announced and is shown separately until it is law. Results assume you are an Ontario resident, a first-time buyer under both the FHSA and HBP rules, and that the contributions are deducted in the 2026 tax year.

This is an estimate for planning, not tax advice or a filed return. Your actual refund depends on your full return, including tax already withheld. Book the call and we'll run the real numbers with you.
